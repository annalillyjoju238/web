Our Story

Tindog was founded after our CEO, Bruno, a very handsome golden retriever, got rejected by a poodle at the park.

Bruno decided that finding love should be easier for every dog. And that is how Tindog was born!

Our Mission

To help every dog find a friend, a playmate, or their one true love, preferably someone who shares their snacks.

Our Vision

A world where no dog has to sit alone by the window watching other dogs go on romantic walks.
